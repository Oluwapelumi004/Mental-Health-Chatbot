Breathing Exercises

Box Breathing

A technique used by Navy SEALs to stay calm under pressure. Great for anxiety and stress.

  1. Inhale slowly for 4 seconds
  2. Hold your breath for 4 seconds
  3. Exhale slowly for 4 seconds
  4. Hold again for 4 seconds

4-7-8 Breathing

A calming technique that activates your body's relaxation response. Ideal before sleep.

  1. Inhale through your nose for 4 seconds
  2. Hold your breath for 7 seconds
  3. Exhale fully through your mouth for 8 seconds
  4. Repeat 3–4 times

5-5 Equal Breathing

Simple and effective. Balances the nervous system and helps you feel grounded quickly.

  1. Inhale slowly for 5 seconds
  2. Exhale slowly for 5 seconds
  3. Focus only on your breath
  4. Repeat for 2–5 minutes

Mental Health Tips

📓

Journal Daily

Writing down your thoughts helps process emotions and gain clarity. Even 5 minutes a day makes a difference.

🚶

Move Your Body

A short walk, stretch, or dance break releases endorphins and can shift your mood significantly.

😴

Prioritise Sleep

Poor sleep worsens anxiety and depression. Aim for 7–9 hours and try a consistent bedtime routine.

📵

Limit Social Media

Constant scrolling increases comparison and anxiety. Try a 30-minute daily limit and notice how you feel.

🤝

Talk to Someone

Sharing your feelings with a trusted friend, family member, or counsellor reduces the weight you carry alone.

🌱

Be Kind to Yourself

You wouldn't speak harshly to a friend going through hard times. Extend that same compassion to yourself.
